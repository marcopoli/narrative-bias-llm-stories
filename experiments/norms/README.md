Human warmth and competence norms for occupational groups in Germany (Friehs et al., 2022, J. Appl. Soc. Psychol.,
https://doi.org/10.1111/jasp.12872), computed from the open data on OSF (https://osf.io/gxz49/, file "Data Stereotype
Content of Occupations (full sample long data format).csv"). Warmth = mean of W1-W4, competence = mean of C1-C4 (1-5
scale), complete answers only. Index1 follows the order of presentation in the English survey (OSM B.2).

occupation_prestige_hughes2022.csv: occupational prestige (0-100) from the Occupational Prestige Ratings of Hughes,
Srivastava, Leszko and Condon (2024, Collabra: Psychology, https://doi.org/10.1525/collabra.92882; data: https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/G1E4BF,
file OccupationalPrestigeRatings.tab, column "OPR Job Rating"). Each story occupation is mapped to the closest OPR job
title(s); where the label covers several titles (teacher, banker) the mean is used. Codes and titles are in the file.
